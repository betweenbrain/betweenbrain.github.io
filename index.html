Matt Thomas

Since 2001, I have been delivering value-added services to solve the needs of organizations and individuals. I founded betweenbrain™ to aid my former colleagues and businesses of all sizes in need of unique problem solving and technology services. I have since provided services from software customization, systems integration, website and web-based technology development and deployment. My core goal is to provide value-added services and web solutions that empower you to be control.

I began working with the Joomla!® content management system in 2006. Since then, I was a team leader in the the Joomla Bug Squad, presenter at numerous Joomla events, and completed a term as a member of the Joomla Production Leadership Team. I'm also the creator of the [now retired] Construct Template Development Framework, Joomla Ajax Interface [now a Joomla core component], and numerous smaller extensions and tools for Joomla and general web development.
